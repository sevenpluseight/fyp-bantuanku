# BantuanKu Government Aid Programs

The program documentation in this directory serves as the reference for the system's government aid discovery, 
rule-based eligibility matching, personalized recommendations and guided application assistance features.

---

## V1.0 Program Database
The initial version of BantuanKu focuses on seven government assistance programs relevant to the financial, 
welfare, healthcare, assistive and housing needs of B40 elderly communities.

## Program Documentation
### 1. Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR)
**Category: Financial Assistance**

STR is a direct financial aid program provided by the Malaysian Government and the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia
(LHDNM) manages the distribution. It aims to help low- and middle-income groups (B40 and lower M40) handle the rising
cost of living.

See [STR Documentation](./aid-programs/str.md)

### 2. Bantuan Warga Emas (BWE)
**Category: Elderly Assistance**
BWE is a monthly financial assistance program provided by the Malaysian government for low-income senior citizens. The
program is managed by the Department of Social Welfare (JKM) under the Ministry of Women, Family and Community Development.
Its main goal is to help elderly individuals living in poverty sustain their daily lives and well-being within the community.

See [BWE Documentation](./aid-programs/bwe.md)

### 3. Bantuan Am Persekutuan (BA)
**Category: General Welfare Assistance**
BA is a social welfare cash assistance scheme managed by Malaysia's Social Welfare Department (JKM). It provides temporary
financial support to low-income, underprivileged individuals and families to help them meet basic living needs and sustain
their livelihoods.

See [BA Documentation](./aid-programs/ba.md)

### 4. Bantuan Perjagaan OKU Terlantar / Pesakit Kronik Terlantar (BPT)
**Category: Care Assistance**
BPT is a monthly financial aid program for caregivers of bedridden individuals and is provided by the Department of Social
Welfare (JKM). The purpose of the program is to recognize caregiver responsibilities and help cover basic daily care and
living costs.

See [BPT Documentation](./aid-programs/bpt.md)

### 5. Bantuan Alat Sokongan / Alat Tiruan (BAT)
**Category: Assistive Support**
It is a financial assistance program provided by the Department of Social Welfare (JKM) to help low-income and eligible 
individuals purchase necessary artificial limbs or supportive equipment. Covered equipments include prosthetic limbs
(artificial legs/arms), manual or electric wheelchairs, hearing aids, white canes and braille machines, etc.

See [BAT Documentation](./aid-programs/bat.md)

### 6. Tabung Bantuan Perubatan (TBP)
**Category: Medical Assistance**
It is a government-backed welfare fund established under Malaysia's Ministry of Health (MOH) to help disadvantaged citizens.
It provides partial or full financial aid to cover medical treatments, medicines and rehabilitation or medical equipment.

See [TBP Documentation](./aid-programs/tbp.md)

The shared eligibility requirements across all programs are documented in [Eligibility Matrix](./aid-programs/eligibility-matrix.md).
